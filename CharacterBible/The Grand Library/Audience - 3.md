# AUDIENCE 3: POMFLOON
**The Shy Specter**

**Fusion:** Aipom x Drifloon  
**Nature:** Timid, Mischievous, Affectionate  

**Appearance**  
PomFloon is a curious, floating little creature.
It has the general shape of an Aipom, but its body is made of soft, ghostly purple Drifloon material, making it lighter than air.
It possesses a little patch of white cloud on its head, and its most distinct feature is its long, string-like tail that ends in a playful, three-fingered hand.
Because it is filled with ghost-energy, PomFloon naturally drifts upward when startled and has to hold onto things to stay grounded.

**Personality and Story**  
At first glance, PomFloon is incredibly timid. Sudden noises, dark shadows, or even a stern look will cause it to whimper and float up to the ceiling in a panic.
However, once it feels safe, a deeply playful, mischievous side emerges. PomFloon loves the Grand Archive, especially when the Archiver shares stories of the [Wind World] and the [Dark World].
It uses its ghost typing to turn invisible, floating above the Archiver's desk to playfully steal a quill or tap Togecik on the shoulder before vanishing again with a soft, echoing giggle. 

**Friendship Dynamic**  
PomFloon relies heavily on its friends to navigate the world. On particularly windy days, PomFloon will wrap its tail-hand firmly around Blulithe's leg or Togecik's shell so it doesn't accidentally drift away into the sky.
While Togecik's loud energy sometimes startles PomFloon, the ghost-monkey absolutely adores the fiery little egg.
When they play together, PomFloon will use its tail to lift Togecik into the air so the flightless bird can experience "flying."
Though it is the most easily frightened of the three, if Togecik or Blulithe are ever genuinely trapped or in danger, PomFloon will swallow its fear, turning invisible to sneak past bullies and pull off daring, mischievous rescues to save its best friends.
