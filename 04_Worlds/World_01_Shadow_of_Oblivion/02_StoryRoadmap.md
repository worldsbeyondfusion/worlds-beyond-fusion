**Theme:** Darkedge (Darkrai x Ceruledge Fusion)
**Tone:** Dark Fantasy, Ideological Conflict, Desperation, Tragic Consequence

## PRODUCTION ROADMAP

### Phase 1: Pre-Production (The LLM Phase)
Before generating video assets, break down each episode into a technical shot list using an LLM. Provide the finalized story and request a tabular output including: **Shot #, Camera Angle, Subject Action, Lighting, and Video AI Prompt Keywords**. Utilize your established naming conventions for prompt files (e.g., `june26scene1prompt`, `june26scene2prompt_v2`, `june26scene3prompt_v2`) to maintain version control and tracking across episodes.

### Phase 3: Post-Production & Assembly (The Edits)
* **Audio is King:** With no dialogue, sound design carries the emotional weight. Lock down tracklists early (music box for Eps 1, choir for Eps 2, cello for Eps 8).
* **Pacing:** Keep Episodes 1-6 tight (45-60 seconds). Let Episode 7 break the rule and breathe for the extended action sequence. Cut Episode 7 abruptly to silence, and let Episode 8 run slow and atmospheric.
