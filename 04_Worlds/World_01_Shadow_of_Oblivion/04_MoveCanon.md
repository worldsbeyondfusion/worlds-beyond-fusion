# Shadow of Oblivion: Move Canon

## Purpose

Define the approved game-based moves and visual combat language
for every important character in Shadow of Oblivion.

Moves listed here are approved references for storyline,
storyboard, image-anchor, and video production.

---

## Research Standard

For every move, record:

- Whether it is signature, strongly associated, or simply learnable
- The game or generation used as the source
- The story function
- The visual interpretation
- Any production risk
- Whether the move is canonically approved for this project
