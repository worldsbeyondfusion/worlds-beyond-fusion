## Status Definitions

- `PLANNED`: Anchor identified but specification is incomplete
- `SPEC READY`: Ready for image generation
- `WAITING`: Waiting for generation credits
- `GENERATED`: Candidates exist but are not reviewed
- `REVIEW`: Under producer and canon-owner review
- `REGENERATE`: Candidate failed and needs another attempt
- `APPROVED`: Final anchor selected
- `VIDEO TEST`: Approved image undergoing motion testing
- `LOCKED`: Approved for production and protected from casual revision
- `ARCHIVED`: No longer active but retained for history

- Act: 2
- Scene: 5

- Froslass
