# Act 6: The Darkedge

**Status:** PROPOSED REVISION
**Target Runtime:** 65–80 seconds
**Planned Scene Action:** 63 seconds (leaves ~2s for visual holds and transitions)
**Scene Count:** 6 scenes

## Act Metadata

*   **Act Purpose:** Formally introduce the project-original fusion "Darkedge." Establish its visual identity, demonstrate the initial instability of two clashing combat instincts, and show them earning perfect coordination through their shared protective drive. Shift the narrative focus from the Vanguard swarm to the god-tier threat of Yveltal.
*   **Audience Knowledge:** Ceruledge and Darkrai have successfully fused into a single entity (Darkedge); the fusion initially struggles to synchronize its movements; the shared instinct to protect the refugees forces them into perfect unity; Darkedge easily destroys the remaining Vanguard; the massive spike in energy alerts Yveltal to their presence.
*   **Primary Character:** Darkedge (Fusion)
*   **Secondary Character:** The Corrupted Vanguard, Froslass, Yveltal
*   **Threat Presence:** The Corrupted Vanguard / Yveltal's Attention

## Scene Breakdown

### Scene 1: The Ethereal Forge

*   **Purpose:** Reveal the physical design of the Darkedge fusion, blending Ceruledge's armor and blades with Darkrai's ethereal, shadowy traits.
*   **Characters:** Darkedge, The Corrupted Vanguard
*   **Primary Character:** Darkedge
*   **Location:** Ruined throne room dais
*   **What Changes:** The chaotic vortex of energy dissipates, leaving a single, powerful entity in its place.
*   **Scene Action:** The towering pillar of violet fire and black shadow suddenly contracts, absorbing into a single point before violently bursting outward in a silent shockwave. As the dust clears, Darkedge stands in the center of the dais. It bears Ceruledge's sleek, armored plating, but the armor is forged of pitch-black, spectral matter. A tattered, smoky cape billows from its shoulders, and its arm-blades burn with intense, dark-violet ethereal fire.
*   **Key Visual:** The first clear, wide shot of Darkedge standing amidst the swirling dust, its violet blades casting an eerie glow on the ruined throne room.
*   **Audience Emotion:** Awe, intense hype, triumph.
*   **Estimated Duration:** 10 seconds

### Scene 2: The Clashing Soul

*   **Purpose:** Establish that the fusion is not instantly perfect; their differing combat instincts create a tactical vulnerability.
*   **Characters:** Darkedge, The Corrupted Vanguard
*   **Primary Character:** Darkedge
*   **Location:** Ruined throne room dais
*   **What Changes:** The Vanguard resumes its attack, exposing the fusion's lack of synchronization.
*   **Scene Action:** The endless tide of Corrupted Vanguard screeches and lunges forward. Darkedge moves to intercept, but stumbles awkwardly. The aggressive, forward-driving instinct of Ceruledge violently conflicts with the evasive, hovering instinct of Darkrai. The fusion's violet flames sputter and flare erratically as the two minds fail to synchronize, leaving a massive opening. A Vanguard attacker slams heavily into Darkedge's shoulder, knocking the fusion off balance.
*   **Key Visual:** Darkedge's violent flames sputtering as it stumbles, taking a heavy hit from a shadowy Vanguard attacker.
*   **Audience Emotion:** Tension, sudden anxiety, realization of vulnerability.
*   **Estimated Duration:** 10 seconds

### Scene 3: Forging the Bond

*   **Purpose:** Show Darkedge earning its power by synchronizing through a shared instinct to protect the survivors.
*   **Characters:** Darkedge, The Corrupted Vanguard, Froslass, The Refugee Crowd
*   **Primary Character:** Darkedge
*   **Location:** Ruined throne room dais
*   **What Changes:** The internal conflict ceases, unlocking their combined potential.
*   **Scene Action:** Knocked backward, Darkedge sees several Vanguard attackers bypassing them, lunging straight for Froslass and the huddling refugees. The internal conflict instantly ceases. Driven by a singular, unified protective instinct, Darkedge stabilizes. The dark aura and violent flames weave together perfectly into a smooth, terrifying violet glow. Darkedge blinks across the dais effortlessly, catching the lead Vanguard by the throat and crushing it into smoke.
*   **Key Visual:** Darkedge stabilizing its aura into a smooth, bright violet glow right before teleporting to protect the crowd.
*   **Audience Emotion:** Relief, hype, satisfaction.
*   **Estimated Duration:** 12 seconds

### Scene 4: The Violet Cull

*   **Purpose:** Demonstrate the overwhelming, newly coordinated combat properties of the fusion.
*   **Characters:** Darkedge, The Corrupted Vanguard
*   **Primary Character:** Darkedge
*   **Location:** Ruined throne room
*   **What Changes:** The Vanguard's numerical advantage is instantly nullified by perfect synergy.
*   **Scene Action:** Now moving in perfect unison, Darkedge blinks rapidly around the room, leaving trails of violet smoke. It perfectly combines Ceruledge's devastating blade arcs with Darkrai's ethereal shadow maneuvers. A massive crescent of void energy sweeps the room from its blades, instantly dissolving dozens of corrupted attackers. The desperate struggle is replaced by surgical dominance, forcing the remaining swarm to scatter.
*   **Key Visual:** Darkedge blinking rapidly around the room, unleashing a massive, glowing crescent of violet and black energy that erases the shadowy Vanguard.
*   **Audience Emotion:** Thrill, vindication.
*   **Estimated Duration:** 10 seconds

### Scene 5: The Silent Guardian

*   **Purpose:** Reassure the audience (and Froslass) that the protective core of Ceruledge and Darkrai survived the fusion process.
*   **Characters:** Darkedge, Froslass, The Refugee Crowd
*   **Primary Character:** Darkedge
*   **Location:** The center of the dais
*   **What Changes:** The terrifying new entity proves it is still a protector to the surviving crowd.
*   **Scene Action:** The throne room falls deathly silent. Darkedge stands amidst the dissipating dark fog. It turns slowly toward Froslass and the surviving refugees, who are huddled next to Solgaleo's petrified statue, trembling at this new monster. Darkedge does not speak. It simply lowers its flaming blades, dims its ethereal aura, and gives Froslass a slow, reassuring nod.
*   **Key Visual:** The terrifying, towering Darkedge gently lowering its weapons and bowing its head slightly to Froslass and the crowd behind her.
*   **Audience Emotion:** Warmth, relief, emotional anchor.
*   **Estimated Duration:** 10 seconds

### Scene 6: The God's Gaze

*   **Purpose:** End the act by shifting the stakes from the Vanguard army to Yveltal itself.
*   **Characters:** Darkedge, Yveltal
*   **Primary Character:** Darkedge / Yveltal
*   **Location:** Ruined throne
