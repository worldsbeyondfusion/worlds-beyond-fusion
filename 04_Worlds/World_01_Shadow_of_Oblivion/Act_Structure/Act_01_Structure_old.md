# Act 1 Structure: The Last Light

## Status

Draft for producer and editorial review.

---

## Act Purpose

Establish the threat of erasure, show Solgaleo preparing to resist it, and lead Darkrai from observation and despair toward discovering the world's last visible refuge.

By the end of the act:

- The catastrophe has begun.
- Solgaleo has recognized the threat and created a barrier around the kingdom.
- Darkrai has witnessed the physical destruction caused by the mist.
- Darkrai has discovered that the mist also erases memories and emotional connections.
- Darkrai has changed from a distant observer into an active participant.
- Darkrai has discovered Solgaleo's protected kingdom.
- Darkrai begins traveling toward the kingdom in search of resistance and possible hope.

---

## Audience Knowledge

By the end of this act, the audience should understand:

1. Solgaleo recognizes the approaching catastrophe before it reaches the kingdom.
2. Solgaleo creates the golden barrier to protect the kingdom.
3. The reddish-black mist destroys physical life and environments.
4. The mist also erases memories and emotional connections.
5. Darkrai considers memory erasure a violation of the balance between light and darkness.
6. Darkrai is angered by the destruction but does not yet know how to stop it.
7. Solgaleo's kingdom appears to be the last place successfully resisting the mist.
8. Darkrai travels toward the kingdom because the golden barrier represents possible hope.

---

## Primary Character

Darkrai

Darkrai owns the emotional journey of the act because:

- Darkrai witnesses the destruction.
- Darkrai investigates the ruins.
- Darkrai discovers the memory erasure.
- Darkrai chooses to stop observing and begin acting.
- Darkrai discovers and approaches Solgaleo's kingdom.

---

## Secondary Character

Solgaleo

Solgaleo establishes the defensive response to the catastrophe by recognizing the danger and creating the golden barrier.

Solgaleo's actions create the final destination of Darkrai's journey through the act.

---

## Threat Presence

Yveltal

Yveltal should remain unclear and distant during this act.

The audience may see:

- An enormous winged silhouette inside the mist.
- Brief fragments of Yveltal's shape.
- The environmental consequences of Yveltal's presence.
- The mist advancing beneath Yveltal's shadow.

Yveltal should not receive a complete visual reveal yet.

# Act 1 Scene Details: The Last Light

## Scene 1: The Premonition

### Purpose

Establish the approaching catastrophe and show that Solgaleo recognizes the danger before the kingdom is directly attacked.

The scene must introduce the threat without completely revealing Yveltal.

### Character

- Solgaleo
- Distant Yveltal silhouette

### Primary Character

Solgaleo

### Location

- Solgaleo's castle balcony
- A distant city beyond the kingdom's territory

### What Changes

Solgaleo's vague premonition becomes visible evidence that the catastrophe has begun.

The danger changes from a possible future event into an active threat.

### Scene Action

Solgaleo stands on the castle balcony and watches the lands beyond the kingdom.

The distant sky begins darkening unnaturally.

A reddish-black mist moves across the mountains and descends toward a distant city.

The mist moves with deliberate and unnatural speed.

A vast winged silhouette briefly forms inside the storm.

The distant city's lights disappear one after another.

Buildings lose their shape beneath the mist.

Solgaleo watches the final light vanish.

The winged silhouette disappears back into the storm before Solgaleo can see its complete form.

Solgaleo turns away from the balcony and moves toward the throne room.

### Key Visual

Solgaleo stands against the kingdom's golden light while a distant city disappears beneath reddish-black mist.

A massive, indistinct winged silhouette briefly forms inside the storm.

### Audience Emotion

Unease and dread.

### Emotional Transition

Solgaleo:

Concern → Recognition → Urgent Resolve

Audience:

Curiosity → Unease → Dread

### Connection from Previous Scene

This is the opening scene of Act 1.

If the Grand Library or narrator framing appears before this scene, the final image from the framing sequence should transition into the distant reddish-black sky above Solgaleo's world.

### Connection to Next Scene

Solgaleo leaves the balcony after realizing that the catastrophe is real.

Solgaleo's recognition of the threat leads directly to the decision to protect the kingdom.

### Estimated Duration

6 seconds

---

## Scene 2: The Barrier

### Purpose

Show Solgaleo responding to the approaching catastrophe and establish the barrier protecting the kingdom.

The scene must present Solgaleo as a prepared and responsible ruler rather than a passive victim.

### Character

- Solgaleo
- Kingdom inhabitants as distant background silhouettes

### Primary Character

Solgaleo

### Location

- Solgaleo's throne room
- The castle
- The surrounding kingdom

### What Changes

Solgaleo's kingdom changes from an exposed territory into a protected sanctuary beneath a golden barrier.

Solgaleo begins sacrificing personal energy to maintain the kingdom's protection.

### Scene Action

Solgaleo enters the throne room and moves toward the center of the castle.

Golden energy begins gathering around Solgaleo's body.

The energy travels through the throne-room floor and spreads across the castle.

Golden lines move through the kingdom's streets, walls, towers, and gates.

The energy reaches the outer boundaries of the kingdom.

A radiant golden dome rises around the entire territory.

The barrier seals above the kingdom.

Outside the barrier, the sky continues darkening.

Inside the barrier, the kingdom remains illuminated by warm golden light.

Solgaleo remains standing in the throne room and continues channeling energy into the barrier.

Solgaleo's glow becomes slightly weaker, foreshadowing the physical cost of maintaining the protection.

Solgaleo is strained but not yet fully exhausted.

### Key Visual

Golden energy travels outward from Solgaleo and forms a radiant dome over the kingdom while the reddish-black sky gathers beyond the barrier.

### Audience Emotion

Temporary relief surrounded by growing dread.

### Emotional Transition

Solgaleo:

Urgent Resolve → Protective Determination → Hidden Strain

Audience:

Dread → Temporary Relief → Concern

### Connection from Previous Scene

Solgaleo has witnessed a distant city disappear and understands that the catastrophe is moving toward the kingdom.

### Connection to Next Scene

The barrier protects Solgaleo's kingdom, but the surrounding cities remain exposed.

The story moves outside the protected territory to show what happens where no barrier exists.

### Estimated Duration

7 seconds

---

## Scene 3: The Forgotten Ruins

### Purpose

Show Darkrai witnessing the physical destruction caused by the reddish-black mist.

The scene introduces Darkrai's perspective and establishes the scale of the catastrophe outside Solgaleo's protected kingdom.

### Character

- Darkrai
- Distant Yveltal silhouette
- Indistinct city inhabitants
- Haunter
- Mimikyu

### Primary Character

Darkrai

### Location

A populated city outside Solgaleo's protected territory.

### What Changes

The city changes from a functioning settlement into lifeless ruins.

Darkrai changes from a distant observer into a direct witness of the destruction.

### Scene Action

Darkrai watches from a high structure overlooking the city.

The reddish-black mist approaches across the surrounding landscape.

A massive winged silhouette moves above the storm.

The complete form remains concealed inside the mist.

The city's inhabitants begin fleeing as the mist reaches the outer streets.

Buildings decay and collapse where the mist passes.

Vegetation blackens.

The city's lights disappear.

Figures caught inside the mist fade before Darkrai can clearly identify what happened to them.

Darkrai continues observing as the mist consumes the settlement.

The winged silhouette follows the movement of the storm and disappears beyond the ruined city.

When the mist moves onward, only damaged buildings, broken streets, and scattered survivors remain.

Darkrai descends from the high structure and enters the destroyed city to investigate.

### Key Visual

Darkrai watches from above as a vast winged shadow passes through the reddish-black mist and the city beneath gradually disappears.

### Audience Emotion

Despair and helplessness.

### Emotional Transition

Darkrai:

Detached Observation → Shock → Disturbed Curiosity

Audience:

Temporary Relief → Horror → Despair

### Connection from Previous Scene

Solgaleo's kingdom has been protected by the golden barrier.

This scene reveals what happens to cities beyond that protection.

### Connection to Next Scene

Darkrai enters the ruins expecting to find only physical destruction.

Inside the destroyed city, Darkrai discovers that the catastrophe has also damaged memory, identity, and emotional connection.

### Estimated Duration

8 seconds

---

## Scene 4: The Breaking Point

### Purpose

Reveal that the mist erases emotional connections and memories, making the catastrophe more disturbing than physical destruction.

The scene must give Darkrai a personal and ideological reason to stop observing and begin acting.

### Character

- Darkrai
- Haunter
- Mimikyu
- Damaged Totodile plushie

### Primary Character

Darkrai

### Location

The destroyed city after the mist has passed.

### What Changes

Darkrai realizes that the mist does not merely destroy physical life.

The mist also erases memory, identity, and emotional connection.

Darkrai changes from shock and despair into controlled anger.

### Scene Action

Darkrai moves silently through the ruined streets.

The environment is damaged, quiet, and almost completely colorless.

Darkrai discovers Haunter sitting among the ruins.

Haunter is holding a damaged Totodile plushie.

Haunter is crying but cannot understand why the plushie matters.

Haunter repeatedly examines the plushie as though attempting to recover a missing memory.

Nearby, Mimikyu stares at a damaged wall without reacting to the destruction surrounding the area.

Mimikyu no longer remembers what previously existed in that location.

Darkrai observes both survivors.

Darkrai looks from Haunter's tears to the forgotten plushie.

Darkrai recognizes that emotional pain remains even though the memory that created the pain has been erased.

Darkrai understands that the mist is destroying the balance between light and darkness by removing the meaning that allows either side to exist.

Nightmare energy begins gathering around Darkrai.

Darkrai turns toward the direction taken by the mist.

Darkrai's passive observation ends.

### Key Visual

Haunter holds the damaged Totodile plushie and cries without understanding the source of the grief.

Darkrai stands nearby as controlled nightmare energy begins forming around Darkrai's body.

### Audience Emotion

Horror, grief, and anger.

### Emotional Transition

Darkrai:

Shock → Despair → Understanding → Controlled Anger

Audience:

Despair → Emotional Horror → Anger

### Connection from Previous Scene

Darkrai has witnessed the city's physical destruction and entered the ruins to investigate what remains.

### Connection to Next Scene

Darkrai searches the horizon for any surviving resistance or remaining source of light.

The discovery of memory erasure gives Darkrai a reason to stop observing and begin moving against the catastrophe.

### Estimated Duration

10 seconds

---

## Scene 5: The Last Light

### Purpose

Give Darkrai and the audience a fragile source of hope.

Establish Solgaleo's protected kingdom as Darkrai's next destination and create the unanswered question that carries the story into Act 2.

### Character

- Darkrai
- Distant Solgaleo kingdom
- Advancing reddish-black mist

### Primary Character

Darkrai

### Location

Ruined high ground overlooking the devastated landscape and the distant kingdom.

### What Changes

Darkrai discovers that one golden light remains untouched by the mist.

Darkrai changes from anger without direction into determined movement toward possible resistance.

### Scene Action

Darkrai reaches high ground above the destroyed city.

The surrounding landscape is covered by ruined settlements, ash, and advancing reddish-black mist.

Darkrai searches the horizon.

Almost every visible settlement has gone dark.

A distant golden light remains visible through the darkness.

The light comes from Solgaleo's barrier.

The golden dome stands alone beneath the darkening sky.

The reddish-black mist surrounds the distant kingdom but has not entered it.

Darkrai recognizes that something inside the kingdom is still resisting the catastrophe.

Darkrai's nightmare energy settles into a controlled form.

Darkrai turns away from the destroyed city.

Darkrai begins moving across the devastated landscape toward the golden barrier.

The barrier flickers briefly in the distance, suggesting that the protection is weakening.

The act ends before Darkrai reaches the kingdom.

### Key Visual

Across the blackened landscape, Darkrai sees Solgaleo's golden barrier glowing alone beneath the reddish-black sky.

### Audience Emotion

Fragile hope and anticipation.

### Emotional Transition

Darkrai:

Controlled Anger → Curiosity → Fragile Hope → Determination

Audience:

Horror → Cautious Relief → Anticipation

### Connection from Previous Scene

Darkrai has discovered that the mist erases memory and emotional meaning.

Darkrai has chosen to stop observing and search for something still capable of resisting the catastrophe.

### Ending and Cliffhanger

Darkrai begins traveling toward the distant golden kingdom.

The barrier flickers briefly.

Darkrai increases speed toward the remaining light.

Cut before Darkrai reaches the barrier.

### Connection to Act 2

Act 2 begins with Ceruledge already racing toward the same kingdom from another direction.

Darkrai approaches because the kingdom represents possible hope and resistance.

Ceruledge returns because the kingdom represents home, loyalty, and duty.

Both characters are moving toward Solgaleo, but Darkrai and Ceruledge have not met.

### Estimated Duration

7 seconds
