## Act Metadata

* **Act Purpose:** Demonstrate that despite their combat synergy, Ceruledge and Darkrai cannot win a war of attrition against an infinite enemy while shielding the massive crowd of surge-protected refugees[span_0](start_span)[span_0](end_span). Push the heroes to their absolute physical and emotional limits, ending with the initial, unexplained spark of their desperate fusion (Darkedge)[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span).

* **Audience Knowledge:** The Corrupted Vanguard is too numerous to defeat conventionally; Ceruledge and Darkrai are fighting tooth and nail to protect both Froslass and the refugee crowd[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span); conventional attacks are failing as exhaustion sets in[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span); an unprecedented phenomenon (fusion resonance) begins when both are pushed to the brink[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).

* **Primary Character:** Ceruledge / Darkrai[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span)

* **Secondary Character:** Froslass, The Surge-Protected Refugees, The Corrupted Vanguard[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span)

## Revision Notes

1. **Scene 4 (The Last Shield):** Choreographed the sequence so Ceruledge and Darkrai independently move to intercept the threat to Froslass and the refugees, converging on the same zone and being overwhelmed together in physical contact[span_105](start_span)[span_105](end_span).
2. **Scene 5 (The Resonance):** Preserved the visual interaction between Ceruledge's violet fire and Darkrai's shadow while explicitly leaving the underlying cause of the fusion unexplained[span_106](start_span)[span_106](end_span). Limited the shockwave's effect so that it repels only the nearest attackers, leaving the larger swarm as an ongoing threat[span_107](start_span)[span_107](end_span).
3. **Scene 6 (The Catalyst):** Streamlined the visual progression to prioritize the immediate pushback of attackers, Froslass and the refugees witnessing the event, the merging of the silhouettes inside the energy pillar, and the concluding dual-toned roar without prematurely revealing Darkedge's full powers or powerset[span_108](start_span)[span_108](end_span).
