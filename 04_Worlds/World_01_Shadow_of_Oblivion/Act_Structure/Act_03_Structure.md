# Act 3: The Misunderstanding

**Status:** GREG APPROVED

**Target Runtime:** 65–80 seconds

**Planned Scene Action:** 63 seconds (leaves ~2s for visual holds and transitions)

**Scene Count:** 6 scenes

## Act Metadata

* **Act Purpose:** Resolve the misunderstanding between Ceruledge and Darkrai through observable actions rather than dialogue. Demonstrate Ceruledge's desperation to protect the surviving crowd and Darkrai's restraint in combat. Present the erasure of a second settlement and establish an uneasy truce as both recognize a massive threat in the sky (Yveltal).

* **Audience Knowledge:** Ceruledge misinterprets Darkrai's anguished roar as a threat; Ceruledge attacks first to protect Froslass and the massive crowd of surge-protected refugees; Darkrai remains focused on the sky and exhibits clear physical restraint; Froslass's grief breaks the conflict; a second distant town is erased by the dark-red mist; Ceruledge and Darkrai spot Yveltal's silhouette and form a silent, provisional alignment out of necessity to protect the throne room.

* **Primary Character:** Ceruledge / Darkrai

* **Secondary Character:** Froslass, Yveltal (silhouette), The Refugee Crowd

* **Threat Presence:** Creeping Dark-Red Mist / Yveltal

## Scene Breakdown

### Scene 1: The Continuing Echo

* **Purpose:** Seamlessly bridge the act boundary from Act 2 and establish Ceruledge's protective suspicion over the refugees.

* **Characters:** Ceruledge, Darkrai, Froslass, The Refugee Crowd

* **Primary Character:** Ceruledge

* **Location:** Ruined throne room

* **What Changes:** Ceruledge locks onto Darkrai as a dangerous unknown threatening the remaining survivors.

* **Scene Action:** Darkrai's anguished roar from Act 2 continues seamlessly across the act boundary. Ceruledge stands fiercely in front of the weeping Froslass, the massive crowd of huddled, surge-protected refugees, and Solgaleo's petrified statue. Surveying the ruined sanctuary and seeing this ominous, roaring Dark-type in the center of the devastation, Ceruledge forms a swift, protective suspicion: this unknown entity is dangerous and must be kept away from the vulnerable crowd.

* **Key Visual:** Ceruledge taking a low defensive stance in front of a sea of terrified refugees, visor locked onto Darkrai's roaring form.

* **Audience Emotion:** Tension, suspense, tragic irony.

* **Emotional Transition:** Shocking grief hardening into swift, protective suspicion.

* **Connection from Previous Scene:** Direct continuation of Act 2, Scene 6 (Darkrai's anguished roar).

* **Connection to Next Scene:** Ceruledge's suspicion leads directly to initiating the attack in Scene 2.

* **Connection to Act 4:** Establishes the volatile dynamic between Ceruledge and Darkrai before the vanguard arrives in Act 4.

* **Estimated Duration:** 10 seconds

### Scene 2: Strike First

* **Purpose:** Show Ceruledge initiating combat out of desperate protective motivation to keep the threat away from the crowd, not to defeat Darkrai for its own sake.

* **Characters:** Ceruledge, Darkrai, The Refugee Crowd (Background)

* **Primary Character:** Ceruledge

* **Location:** Ruined throne room dais

* **What Changes:** Tense suspicion erupts into a physical confrontation.

* **Scene Action:** Unwilling to let the roaring entity take a single step closer to the terrified refugees, Ceruledge ignites both arm blades with bright flame and lunges forward in a high-speed strike to force the entity backward. Darkrai, still fixated on the collapsing ceiling and the dark-red sky above, is caught off-guard by the sudden, aggressive assault from inside the hall.

* **Key Visual:** Dual burning blades cutting through drifting dust as Ceruledge closes the distance toward Darkrai, leaving the huddled crowd in the background.

* **Audience Emotion:** Adrenaline, urgency, apprehension.

* **Emotional Transition:** Defensive fear escalating into desperate aggression.

* **Connection from Previous Scene:** Follows immediately from Ceruledge's protective stance in Scene 1.

* **Connection to Next Scene:** Escalates into the physical parrying in Scene 3.

* **Connection to Act 4:** Demonstrates Ceruledge's combat readiness and self-sacrificing instinct.

* **Estimated Duration:** 10 seconds

### Scene 3: Conflicting Priorities

* **Purpose:** Highlight Darkrai's spatial awareness to avoid hurting the refugees and his distraction with the sky, demonstrating restraint through observable action.

* **Characters:** Ceruledge, Darkrai, The Refugee Crowd (Background)

* **Primary Character:** Darkrai

* **Location:** Ruined throne room floor

* **What Changes:** The clash visually reveals a fundamental mismatch in priorities between the two combatants.

* **Scene Action:** Ceruledge presses a rapid series of blade strikes to push the entity away from the crowd. Darkrai glides backward effortlessly, parrying the blows with dark, ethereal energy. Darkrai visibly angles his deflections to ensure sparks and energy do not hit the cowering refugees behind Ceruledge. Instead of striking back, Darkrai’s single eye repeatedly darts away from Ceruledge, looking anxiously back up toward the dark-red storm clouds. 

* **Key Visual:** Darkrai deflecting a flaming blade swipe while gazing anxiously over Ceruledge's shoulder at the crimson clouds, angling the impact away from the refugees.

* **Audience Emotion:** Heightened tension, realization of Darkrai's true focus.

* **Emotional Transition:** Desperate aggression met with distracted, defensive restraint.

* **Connection from Previous Scene:** Direct physical consequence of Ceruledge's strike in Scene 2.

* **Connection to Next Scene:** Provides the groundwork for Darkrai's explicit act of restraint in Scene 4.

* **Connection to Act 4:** Establishes Darkrai's defense-focused fighting style.

* **Estimated Duration:** 12 seconds

### Scene 4: Observable Restraint

* **Purpose:** Provide clear physical evidence of Darkrai's priorities. Darkrai actively shields the crowd, giving Ceruledge reason to question his initial assumption.

* **Characters:** Ceruledge, Darkrai, Froslass, The Refugee Crowd

* **Primary Character:** Darkrai / Ceruledge

* **Location:** Ruined throne room floor

* **What Changes:** Ceruledge questions his assumption after Darkrai chooses to shield the survivors rather than counter-attack.

* **Scene Action:** Ceruledge overextends on a heavy downward strike. Darkrai catches Ceruledge's burning wrist, easily stopping the blade. Suddenly, a loud crack echoes from above as a heavy stone beam breaks from the ruined ceiling, falling directly toward Froslass and the front rows of the refugees. Instead of using his free hand to strike the exposed Ceruledge, Darkrai shoves Ceruledge safely backward and throws his hand upward, raising a massive shadowy barrier that catches the falling debris.

* **Key Visual:** Darkrai shoving Ceruledge back safely with one hand while casting a wide shadow shield over the terrified crowd to catch the falling stone.

* **Audience Emotion:** Surprise, cognitive relief.

* **Emotional Transition:** Aggressive momentum halted by sudden, confused realization.

* **Connection from Previous Scene:** Direct culmination of the combat exchange in Scene 3.

* **Connection to Next Scene:** Halts the fight, allowing Froslass to step in and shift focus in Scene 5.

* **Connection to Act 4:** Breaks down the hostility between the two, laying the foundation for cooperative defense.

* **Estimated Duration:** 10 seconds

### Scene 5: The Second Erasure

* **Purpose:** End the fight through Froslass's emotional intervention and reveal the ongoing scale of the tragedy to the surviving crowd.

* **Characters:** Froslass, Ceruledge, Darkrai, The Refugee Crowd

* **Primary Character:** Froslass

* **Location:** Ruined throne room overlook / ledge

* **What Changes:** Internal conflict ceases entirely; complete focus shifts back to the external catastrophe.

* **Scene Action:** Froslass glides between Ceruledge and Darkrai, silently shaking her head with icy tears in her eyes. Ceruledge lowers his blades, flames dimming as he questions his swift judgment. Darkrai turns and points out toward the horizon through the shattered wall. The surviving crowd of refugees gasps collectively: far in the distance, a second peaceful settlement is quietly swallowed and erased by the creeping dark-red mist.

* **Key Visual:** Ceruledge and Darkrai standing beside Froslass at the broken edge, watching a distant town vanish, while the massive crowd behind them watches in silent horror.

* **Audience Emotion:** Heavy sorrow, chilling dread.

* **Emotional Transition:** Hostility dissolving into profound, shared dread.

* **Connection from Previous Scene:** Follows Darkrai's act of restraint in Scene 4.

* **Connection to Next Scene:** Unites their focus toward the sky where a new shape appears in Scene 6.

* **Connection to Act 4:** Confirms the widespread reach of the mist across the world.

* **Estimated Duration:** 13 seconds

### Scene 6: The Shadow in the Sky

* **Purpose:** Reveal a looming threat in the sky, forcing an uneasy truce out of necessity.

* **Characters:** Ceruledge, Darkrai, Yveltal (silhouette), The Refugee Crowd

* **Primary Character:** Ceruledge / Darkrai

* **Location:** Ruined throne room overlook

* **What Changes:** An implicit, provisional alliance is forged out of necessity to protect the room from an approaching threat.

* **Scene Action:** High above the newly erased settlement, the dark-red storm clouds part briefly. The colossal, unmistakable silhouette of Yveltal glides silently through the upper atmosphere. The refugees whimper and shrink back toward Solgaleo's statue. Ceruledge looks up, recognizing the ominous shape from an earlier glimpse, and realizes a much greater danger approaches. He glances at Darkrai. Out of sheer necessity, the two warriors step forward side-by-side, forming a silent, provisional vanguard between the sky and the crowd.

* **Key Visual:** Yveltal's massive silhouette framed against the crimson sky, with Ceruledge and Darkrai standing as a tiny but resolute frontline protecting the massive crowd behind them.

* **Audience Emotion:** Chilling awe, solemn resolve, excitement for the coming fight.

* **Emotional Transition:** Shared dread turning into cold, united determination.

* **Connection from Previous Scene:** Direct continuation as all three look out at the erased settlement from Scene 5.

* **Connection to Next Scene:** Concludes Act 3 on an emotional cliffhanger; sets up Act 4 for the arrival of Yveltal's corrupted vanguard.

* **Connection to Act 4:** Formally bridges the saga into Act 4 where the corrupted vanguard arrives to challenge the newly formed alliance.

* **Estimated Duration:** 8 seconds
