# Act 10: What Victory Leaves Behind

**Status:** PROPOSED REVISION
**Target Runtime:** 65–80 seconds
**Planned Scene Action:** 63 seconds (leaves ~2s for visual holds and transitions)
**Scene Count:** 6 scenes

## Act Metadata

*   **Act Purpose:** Explain the seal, execute the ultimate sacrifice, and establish the permanent consequences of the battle. Prove that the containment prevents further spread but restores nothing, leaving the survivors in a permanently scarred world.
*   **Audience Knowledge:** Darkedge deliberately chooses to act as a sacrificial lock to stop the unanchored mist; Yveltal and the Oblivion force are pulled into a void-seal; Ceruledge and Darkrai do not survive in their normal forms, becoming a petrified obsidian statue; the refugee crowd survives, with Froslass stepping forward as the central witness and eternal keeper of the sealed ruins.
*   **Primary Character:** Darkedge
*   **Secondary Character:** Froslass, The Refugee Crowd, (Sealed Yveltal)
*   **Threat Presence:** The Expanding Mist (Oblivion)

## Scene Breakdown

### Scene 1: The Ethereal Matrix

*   **Purpose:** Darkedge shifts tactics from combat to containment, using established abilities (binding/shadow and spiritual fire) to lock the spread.
*   **Characters:** Darkedge, The Expanding Mist
*   **Primary Character:** Darkedge
*   **Location:** The epicenter of the ruined dais
*   **What Changes:** The battle changes from a clash of force to a sacrificial binding ritual.
*   **Scene Action:** Standing in the heart of the storm, Darkedge plunges its bare, spectral hands directly into the swirling, expanding mist. It manipulates Darkrai's signature shadow-binding and fuses it with Ceruledge's spectral ghost-fire. Violet and black geometric lines explode outward, weaving a massive, intricate containment matrix that physically restrains the mist from reaching the refugees.
*   **Key Visual:** Darkedge anchoring itself in the storm, its arms buried in the mist as a massive, glowing violet sealing-matrix forms around the dais.
*   **Audience Emotion:** Shock, realization of the sacrifice, profound awe.
*   **Emotional Transition:** Desperate realization shifting into a calm, resolute sacrifice.
*   **Connection from Previous Scene:** Direct continuation of Darkedge walking into the expanding mist at the end of Act 9.
*   **Connection to Next Scene:** The sealing process demands its physical toll in Scene 2.
*   **Estimated Duration:** 11 seconds

### Scene 2: The Final Burn

*   **Purpose:** Visually confirm the death/sacrifice of the base characters within the fusion.
*   **Characters:** Ceruledge (Soul), Darkrai (Soul)
*   **Primary Character:** Darkedge
*   **Location:** Inside the Ethereal Matrix
*   **What Changes:** The life forces of Ceruledge and Darkrai are entirely consumed to power the lock.
*   **Scene Action:** The camera pushes in close on Darkedge as the sealing matrix tightens. The matrix requires a permanent anchor, and the fusion's physical armor begins to burn away into ash under the strain. Inside the blinding violet light, the translucent silhouettes of Ceruledge and Darkrai are briefly visible side-by-side. They look at each other, giving a final, synchronized nod of respect, before their glowing forms shatter into countless sparks of light to permanently lock the seal.
*   **Key Visual:** The glowing, ethereal silhouettes of Ceruledge and Darkrai fading into sparks of light inside the roaring violet furnace of the seal.
*   **Audience Emotion:** Heartbreak, profound respect, sorrow.
*   **Emotional Transition:** Resolute sacrifice turning into a beautiful, tragic farewell.
*   **Connection from Previous Scene:** The internal toll of the matrix established in Scene 1.
*   **Connection to Next Scene:** The completion of the seal.
*   **Estimated Duration:** 10 seconds

### Scene 3: The Obsidian Anchor

*   **Purpose:** Conclude the climax. The threat is sealed, and the physical remnants of the heroes are left behind in front of the survivors.
*   **Characters:** The Seal, The Refugee Crowd
*   **Primary Character:** The Seal
*   **Location:** Ruined throne room dais
*   **What Changes:** The Oblivion force is pulled into the void; the active threat is neutralized.
*   **Scene Action:** With the life forces consumed, the geometric seal snaps shut. The rapidly expanding mist is violently sucked backward into a singularity on the dais floor. The singularity implodes with a silent flash of light. When the dust clears, the mist is gone. In the center of the room stands a massive, jagged spike of pitch-black obsidian glass—the eternal lock. The surviving refugees watch in stunned silence.
*   **Key Visual:** A blinding implosion shrinking into a single point, leaving behind a jagged, smoking obsidian spike driven deep into the shattered dais in front of the surviving crowd.
*   **Audience Emotion:** Stunning silence, heavy realization.
*   **Emotional Transition:** Tragic farewell settling into cold, permanent stillness.
*   **Connection from Previous Scene:** The physical result of the final burn in Scene 2.
*   **Connection to Next Scene:** The atmospheric cooldown of the environment.
*   **Estimated Duration:** 11 seconds

### Scene 4: The Dissipating Clouds

*   **Purpose:** Demonstrate that the active threat is contained, but the damage is permanent.
*   **Characters:** The Refugee Crowd
*   **Primary Character:** The Environment
*   **Location:** Ruined throne room
*   **What Changes:** The immediate danger clears, but no miraculous restoration occurs.
*   **Scene Action:** The residual ambient fog slowly dissipates, rolling away from the outer walls. The sky clears of the swirling black storm, but there is no miraculous restoration. The kingdom remains a shattered, grey ruin. The erased Pokémon do not return. The world is safe from further erasure, but the scars left behind are absolute. 
*   **Key Visual:** A wide pan over the leveled, grey ruins of the kingdom, showing the scale of the permanent destruction now that the fog has lifted.
*   **Audience Emotion:** Somber acceptance, heavy relief.
*   **Emotional Transition:** Cold stillness softening into heavy, grounded reality.
*   **Connection from Previous Scene:** The environmental aftermath of the seal closing.
*   **Connection to Next Scene:** Allows Froslass to step forward into the quiet ruins.
*   **Estimated Duration:** 10 seconds

### Scene 5: The Keeper of the Void

*   **Purpose:** Focus on Froslass as the central witness and primary mourner stepping forward from the surviving crowd.
*   **Characters:** Froslass, The Refugee Crowd
*   **Primary Character:** Froslass
*   **Location:** Ruined throne room dais
*   **What Changes:** Froslass accepts her new role as the guardian of the sealed ruins alongside the remaining survivors.
*   **Scene Action:** From the huddled mass of surviving dual-element refugees behind Solgaleo's white-stone legs, Froslass slowly floats out. The throne room is eerily quiet. She glides over to the massive obsidian spike. Embedded in the base of the black glass are the burnt, fractured remnants of Ceruledge's blades. Froslass kneels before the seal, bowing her head in profound, heavy grief on behalf of the survivors.
*   **Key Visual:** Froslass kneeling in the ashes before the jagged obsidian spike, with the burnt remnants of Ceruledge's blades resting at its base, while the refugee crowd watches quietly in the background.
*   **Audience Emotion:** Bittersweet grief, quiet reverence.
*   **Emotional Transition:** Grounded reality focusing into profound, personal gratitude.
*   **Connection from Previous Scene:** The character response to the quiet aftermath.
*   **Connection to Next Scene:** Leads to the final visual motif of the saga.
*   **Estimated Duration:** 11 seconds

### Scene 6: The Crimson Vigil

*   **Purpose:** End the saga on a somber visual, proving the world is permanently scarred.
*   **Characters:** Froslass, (Petrified Solgaleo), (Obsidian Seal)
*   **Primary Character:** The Environment
*   **Location:** Ruined throne room (Vista)
*   **What Changes:** The story concludes under the light of the blood moon.
*   **Scene Action:** As Froslass kneels, the camera tilts upward past the ruined walls. High above the shattered kingdom, the clouds drift apart to reveal the blood moon still hanging ominously in the sky. The camera slowly pulls back, leaving Froslass kneeling in the eerie crimson light. The battle is over, but the world will never be the same.
*   **Key Visual:** The blood moon casting a stark, red light over the twin monuments—Solgaleo's white stone and the dark obsidian spike—as Froslass kneels in the permanently ruined throne room.
*   **Audience Emotion:** Melancholy, restrained relief, tragic closure.
*   **Emotional Transition:** Personal gratitude elevating into a poetic, haunting resolution.
*   **Connection from Previous Scene:** Matches the emotional stillness of Scene 5 with an environmental final shot.
*   **Connection to Next Scene:** N/A (The core narrative of the World is resolved).
*   **Estimated Duration:** 10 seconds
