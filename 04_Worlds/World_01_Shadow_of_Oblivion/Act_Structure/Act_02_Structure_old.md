# Act Structure

## Act Purpose

What must change by the end of this act?
Transform Ceruledge from a returning knight into the guardian of the survivors, complete Solgaleo's sacrifice, and push Darkrai beyond restraint after witnessing the erasure of Togepi.

## Audience Knowledge

What does the audience understand now that the audience did not understand before?
- Ceruledge is loyal to Solgaleo and the kingdom.
- Solgaleo has nearly exhausted himself sustaining the barrier.
- Solgaleo's final power can preserve some beings, but cannot save everyone.
- Darkrai's rage comes from witnessing the destruction of innocence and balance.

## Primary Character

Whose decisions drive this act?
Perspective character: Ceruledge
Decisive character: Solgaleo
Emotional endpoint: Darkrai

## Primary Emotion

What should the viewer mainly feel?
Loyalty, despair

## Scene Limit

Target: 6
Maximum: 8

# Act 2 Scene Details: The Last Grace

## Scene 1: The Desperate Return

### Purpose

Reintroduce Ceruledge as a loyal defender returning to the kingdom after witnessing the approaching catastrophe.

The scene must establish urgency without repeating the destruction already shown in Act 1.

### Character

- Ceruledge
- Distant kingdom inhabitants
- Advancing reddish-black mist

### Primary Character

Ceruledge

### Location

- Devastated land outside Solgaleo's kingdom
- The outer edge of the golden barrier
- The kingdom entrance

### What Changes

Ceruledge moves from patrolling the outer territory to urgently returning home.

Ceruledge crosses from the exposed wasteland into the final protected sanctuary.

### Scene Action

Ceruledge races across the damaged landscape toward Solgaleo's kingdom.

The reddish-black mist advances behind Ceruledge and consumes the terrain along the route.

Ceruledge does not stop to investigate because the scale of the destruction is already clear.

The golden barrier appears ahead, glowing against the darkened sky.

The barrier flickers as Ceruledge approaches.

A temporary opening forms near the kingdom entrance.

Ceruledge passes through the barrier moments before the opening closes.

The reddish-black mist strikes the outside of the golden dome but does not immediately penetrate it.

Ceruledge pauses briefly after entering and looks back at the mist spreading across the outer world.

Ceruledge then turns toward the castle and continues running.

### Key Visual

Ceruledge races through a temporary opening in the golden barrier while the reddish-black mist closes in from behind.

### Audience Emotion

Urgency and temporary relief.

### Emotional Transition

Ceruledge:

Alarm → Urgency → Brief Relief → Renewed Concern

Audience:

Anticipation → Fear → Temporary Relief → Concern

### Connection from Previous Act

Act 1 ends with Darkrai traveling toward Solgaleo's distant golden barrier.

Act 2 begins with Ceruledge approaching the same kingdom from another direction.

Darkrai moves toward the kingdom because the barrier represents possible hope.

Ceruledge returns because the kingdom represents home, duty, and loyalty.

### Connection to Next Scene

Ceruledge enters the kingdom expecting to find Solgaleo maintaining control.

Ceruledge instead discovers that the barrier has already taken a severe physical toll on the Sun King.

### Estimated Duration

6 seconds

---

## Scene 2: The Exhausted King

### Purpose

Reveal the personal cost of maintaining the barrier.

The scene must allow Ceruledge to understand that Solgaleo has been protecting the kingdom continuously since the catastrophe began.

### Character

- Ceruledge
- Solgaleo
- Froslass
- Togepi
- Remaining survivors

### Primary Character

Ceruledge

### Location

Solgaleo's throne room.

### What Changes

Ceruledge realizes that Solgaleo has already sacrificed much of Solgaleo's strength to maintain the barrier.

Ceruledge's expectation of finding a powerful ruler changes into concern for a visibly weakened king.

### Scene Action

Ceruledge enters the throne room.

Golden energy runs through the floor and walls toward the barrier surrounding the kingdom.

Solgaleo stands at the center of the throne room, continuing to channel power into the protection.

Solgaleo's golden light is weaker and less stable than it was during Act 1.

Ceruledge slows after seeing Solgaleo's condition.

Solgaleo struggles to remain standing but refuses to release the barrier.

Froslass remains inside a protected alcove with Togepi and several survivors.

The survivors look toward Solgaleo as the final source of protection.

Ceruledge looks at the weakening golden energy moving through the room.

Ceruledge understands that Solgaleo has been sustaining the barrier while the outside world was being destroyed.

Ceruledge moves closer to Solgaleo but cannot replace Solgaleo's role in maintaining the protection.

### Key Visual

Ceruledge enters the throne room and finds Solgaleo barely standing beneath unstable golden light while energy continues flowing from Solgaleo into the kingdom's barrier.

### Audience Emotion

Concern, respect, and approaching dread.

### Emotional Transition

Ceruledge:

Urgency → Shock → Concern → Helpless Respect

Solgaleo:

Hidden Strain → Visible Exhaustion → Continued Resolve

Audience:

Temporary Relief → Concern → Dread

### Connection from Previous Scene

Ceruledge has crossed into the protected kingdom and rushed toward the castle.

### Connection to Next Scene

While Ceruledge focuses on Solgaleo, Darkrai reaches the kingdom and enters the damaged castle unnoticed.

Darkrai becomes a silent witness before the final tragedy begins.

### Estimated Duration

7 seconds

---

## Scene 3: The Silent Witness

### Purpose

Place Darkrai inside the castle before Solgaleo's final sacrifice and Togepi's erasure.

The scene must prevent Darkrai from appearing suddenly at the climax.

### Character

- Darkrai
- Solgaleo
- Ceruledge
- Froslass
- Togepi
- Remaining survivors

### Primary Character

Darkrai

### Location

- The outer edge of the weakening barrier
- A damaged castle corridor
- The entrance to the throne room

### What Changes

Darkrai reaches the remaining source of golden light and discovers what Solgaleo is trying to protect.

Darkrai changes from searching for resistance to silently witnessing the kingdom's final defense.

### Scene Action

Darkrai reaches the outer boundary of Solgaleo's kingdom.

The golden barrier flickers again as the reddish-black mist presses against it.

Darkrai passes through a damaged section before the opening closes.

Darkrai moves through the castle without announcing Darkrai's presence.

The corridors are partially damaged and filled with unstable golden light.

Darkrai reaches a dark corridor overlooking the throne room.

From the shadows, Darkrai sees Solgaleo struggling to maintain the barrier.

Darkrai sees Ceruledge standing near the exhausted king.

Darkrai also sees Froslass holding Togepi inside the protected alcove.

The survivors remain focused on Solgaleo and do not notice Darkrai.

Darkrai understands that the golden light seen from the ruined city came from Solgaleo's continued resistance.

Darkrai remains hidden, watching to understand whether the protection can survive.

### Key Visual

Darkrai watches from a dark corridor while Solgaleo radiates unstable golden light in the throne room and Froslass holds Togepi near the survivors.

### Audience Emotion

Unease and anticipation.

### Emotional Transition

Darkrai:

Determination → Cautious Hope → Concern

Audience:

Dread → Brief Hope → Suspense

### Connection from Previous Scene

Ceruledge has discovered that Solgaleo is nearly exhausted from maintaining the barrier.

At the same time, Darkrai reaches the kingdom after following the final golden light from Act 1.

### Connection to Next Scene

The barrier begins failing before Darkrai can approach or communicate.

Solgaleo realizes that maintaining the dome is no longer possible and prepares one final act of protection.

### Estimated Duration

6 seconds

---

## Scene 4: The Last Grace

### Purpose

Complete Solgaleo's sacrifice and transfer responsibility for the survivors to Ceruledge.

The scene must establish that Solgaleo deliberately changes strategy because the barrier can no longer be sustained.

### Character

- Solgaleo
- Ceruledge
- Froslass
- Togepi
- Darkrai
- Remaining survivors

### Primary Character

Solgaleo

### Location

Solgaleo's throne room and the kingdom beneath the failing barrier.

### What Changes

Solgaleo stops using the remaining power to maintain the failing dome.

Solgaleo converts the remaining divine energy into a final protective shockwave.

The barrier collapses.

Solgaleo becomes petrified.

Ceruledge inherits responsibility for the survivors.

### Scene Action

Cracks of reddish-black energy begin spreading across the golden barrier.

The throne room shakes as the mist presses against the protection.

Solgaleo's legs begin giving way beneath the strain.

Ceruledge moves toward Solgaleo, but Solgaleo prevents Ceruledge from interrupting the final action.

Solgaleo looks toward Ceruledge and then toward Froslass, Togepi, and the remaining survivors.

Solgaleo recognizes that the barrier cannot survive another direct impact.

Solgaleo releases the energy sustaining the outer dome.

The golden barrier breaks apart above the kingdom.

Before the reddish-black mist can completely enter, Solgaleo draws the remaining divine energy inward.

Solgaleo releases a monumental roar.

A concentrated shockwave of golden light erupts through the throne room and spreads across the kingdom.

The light passes through Ceruledge and Froslass, anchoring their dual Ghost-type forms against the erasure.

Darkrai shields Darkrai's eyes from the intensity but remains hidden inside the corridor.

The final wave leaves Solgaleo without energy.

Solgaleo's golden body rapidly loses color.

Stone spreads across Solgaleo's body.

Solgaleo becomes a silent grey statue at the center of the throne room.

Ceruledge kneels before the petrified king.

### Key Visual

Solgaleo unleashes a final explosion of divine golden light before the brightness collapses and leaves the Sun King completely petrified.

### Audience Emotion

Awe, grief, and helplessness.

### Emotional Transition

Solgaleo:

Exhaustion → Acceptance → Final Resolve → Silence

Ceruledge:

Concern → Alarm → Awe → Grief

Darkrai:

Cautious Hope → Shock → Recognition of Sacrifice

Audience:

Dread → Awe → Devastation

### Connection from Previous Scene

Darkrai has reached the throne room as a silent witness.

The barrier begins failing while Solgaleo is already severely weakened.

### Connection to Next Scene

Solgaleo's final light protects Ceruledge and Froslass, but the protection does not save everyone.

As the golden light fades, Togepi begins disappearing inside Froslass's embrace.

### Estimated Duration

10 seconds

---

## Scene 5: The Empty Embrace

### Purpose

Reveal the limit of Solgaleo's sacrifice and create the emotional breaking point of the act.

The scene must show that even divine sacrifice cannot completely prevent the erasure.

### Character

- Froslass
- Togepi
- Ceruledge
- Darkrai
- Petrified Solgaleo
- Remaining survivors

### Primary Character

Froslass

### Location

Solgaleo's throne room following the final golden shockwave.

### What Changes

Froslass survives Solgaleo's final protection, but Togepi does not.

The throne room changes from a temporary sanctuary into a place of irreversible loss.

Darkrai's anger changes from controlled determination into an uncontrollable breaking point.

### Scene Action

The golden shockwave fades from the throne room.

Froslass remains kneeling inside the protected alcove while holding Togepi.

Togepi's body begins breaking into small particles of fading light.

Froslass tightens the embrace, but the particles continue escaping between Froslass's arms.

Ceruledge turns away from Solgaleo's statue and sees Togepi disappearing.

Darkrai remains frozen inside the corridor, watching the erasure.

Togepi reaches toward Froslass before the remaining physical form dissolves.

The final particles disappear.

Froslass remains in the same position with both arms wrapped around empty space.

No immediate attack follows.

No character speaks.

Ceruledge looks between Froslass and the petrified Solgaleo.

Darkrai recognizes that Solgaleo sacrificed everything and still could not preserve the innocent life inside the sanctuary.

Nightmare energy begins gathering violently around Darkrai.

### Key Visual

Froslass remains kneeling with both arms wrapped around empty space while the final particles of Togepi disappear.

The petrified Solgaleo stands in the background.

### Audience Emotion

Grief, helplessness, and emotional shock.

### Emotional Transition

Froslass:

Protective Fear → Desperation → Disbelief → Grief

Ceruledge:

Grief for Solgaleo → Shock → Protective Anger

Darkrai:

Shock → Grief → Breaking Rage

Audience:

Devastation → Silence → Dread

### Connection from Previous Scene

Solgaleo's final golden light has protected Ceruledge and Froslass.

The fading light reveals that the protection was incomplete and could not save Togepi.

### Connection to Next Scene

Darkrai can no longer remain silent after witnessing both Solgaleo's sacrifice and Togepi's erasure.

Darkrai's grief erupts into a violent roar.

Ceruledge hears the roar without understanding its cause.

### Estimated Duration

9 seconds

---

## Scene 6: The Nightmare's Roar

### Purpose

Push Darkrai beyond restraint and create the misunderstanding that begins Act 3.

The scene must allow the same roar to carry two different meanings:

- To Darkrai, the roar expresses grief, rage, and a declaration of revenge.
- To Ceruledge, the roar appears to confirm that a nightmare entity has entered the ruined sanctuary.

### Character

- Darkrai
- Ceruledge
- Froslass
- Petrified Solgaleo
- Remaining survivors

### Primary Character

Darkrai

### Location

Solgaleo's throne room and the dark corridor.

### What Changes

Darkrai changes from a silent witness into an openly hostile presence.

Ceruledge becomes aware that an unknown nightmare entity is inside the throne room.

The act moves from shared tragedy into dangerous misunderstanding.

### Scene Action

Darkrai's nightmare energy expands through the corridor.

The dark energy reacts violently against the fading remnants of Solgaleo's golden light.

Darkrai steps partially out of the shadows.

Darkrai releases a massive roar of grief and rage.

The roar shakes the throne room.

Dust and loose fragments fall from the ceiling.

Froslass remains holding the empty space where Togepi disappeared.

Ceruledge rises from beside the petrified Solgaleo.

Ceruledge turns toward the sound.

From Ceruledge's perspective, Darkrai is visible only as a dark figure surrounded by unstable nightmare energy.

Ceruledge sees:

1. The ruined throne room.
2. The petrified Solgaleo.
3. Froslass holding empty space.
4. Darkrai emerging from the corridor.
5. Nightmare energy spreading from Darkrai.
6. Darkrai roaring inside the broken sanctuary.

Ceruledge does not know that Darkrai witnessed or opposed the catastrophe.

Ceruledge moves between Darkrai and the survivors.

Ceruledge's spectral blades begin igniting.

The act cuts before Ceruledge attacks.

### Key Visual

Darkrai roars from the dark corridor while Ceruledge rises beside the petrified Solgaleo and turns toward the nightmare energy.

Froslass remains between them, holding empty space.

### Audience Emotion

Rage, dread, and anticipation.

### Emotional Transition

Darkrai:

Breaking Grief → Uncontrolled Rage → Determination for Revenge

Ceruledge:

Grief → Alarm → Suspicion → Protective Determination

Audience:

Grief → Danger → Anticipation

### Connection from Previous Scene

Togepi has disappeared despite Solgaleo's final sacrifice.

Darkrai reaches the emotional breaking point after witnessing the destruction of both divine resistance and innocent life.

### Ending and Cliffhanger

Ceruledge steps between Darkrai and the survivors.

Both spectral blades ignite.

Darkrai remains surrounded by unstable nightmare energy.

Ceruledge lowers into a defensive stance.

Cut before Ceruledge launches the first attack.

### Connection to Act 3

Act 3 begins from Ceruledge's interpretation of Darkrai's roar.

Ceruledge believes Darkrai may be responsible for:

- Solgaleo's petrification
- Togepi's disappearance
- The collapse of the barrier
- The destruction outside the kingdom

Ceruledge confronts Darkrai to protect the remaining survivors.

Darkrai remains focused on leaving the castle and pursuing Yveltal.

Their conflicting interpretations create the misunderstanding.

### Estimated Duration

7 seconds
