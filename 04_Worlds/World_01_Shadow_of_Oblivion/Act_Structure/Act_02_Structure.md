## Act Metadata

* **Act Purpose:** Detail the failure of the Golden Kingdom's barrier, demonstrate Solgaleo's physical collapse and final petrification sacrifice to save selected refugees, depict Togepi's irreversible loss, establish Darkrai's independent arrival to investigate the breach, and set up Ceruledge's mistaken suspicion leading into Act 3.

* **Audience Knowledge:** Ceruledge returns independently to the kingdom seeking refuge[span_0](start_span)[span_0](end_span); Solgaleo reaches his absolute physical limit maintaining the barrier over a crowd of selected refugees[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span); Darkrai arrives separately, focused intently on analyzing the external threat[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span); the barrier shatters completely[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span); Solgaleo releases a golden surge that creates a survival exception for specific dual-type Pokémon before petrifying[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span); Togepi is not protected and dissolves into the dark-red mist[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span); Darkrai unleashes an anguished roar that Ceruledge misinterprets as hostile intent[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span).

* **Primary Character:** Solgaleo / Ceruledge[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span)

* **Secondary Character:** Froslass, Togepi, Darkrai, The Selected Refugees[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span)

* **Threat Presence:** Creeping Dark-Red Mist (Oblivion)[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span)
