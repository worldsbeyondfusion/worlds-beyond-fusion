# Act 7: The God's Descent

**Status:** APPROVED STORY BY GREG

**Target Runtime:** 65–80 seconds

**Planned Scene Action:** 63 seconds (leaves ~2s for visual holds and transitions)

**Scene Count:** 6 scenes

## Act Metadata

* **Act Purpose:** Establish Yveltal’s unquestionable dominance and observational intelligence. Demonstrate that while Darkedge is growing in power, it is inexperienced and vastly outmatched. Introduce the lethal cost of fighting a god near vulnerable survivors, and plant the crystal Easter egg seamlessly within the environmental destruction.

* **Audience Knowledge:** Yveltal has descended to the battlefield; Darkedge struggles to control its newly fused abilities; Yveltal is not fighting for its life, but observing and dictating the pace; the collateral damage of the fight is deadly to the refugees; Darkedge is forced to defend the survivors against a massive, impending attack.

* **Primary Character:** Darkedge / Yveltal

* **Secondary Character:** Froslass, The Surge-Protected Refugees

* **Threat Presence:** Yveltal (The God of Destruction)

## Scene Breakdown

### Scene 1: The Touchdown

* **Purpose:** Showcase Yveltal's overwhelming physical presence and the immediate environmental danger it poses to the survivors.
* **Characters:** Yveltal, Darkedge, Froslass, The Surge-Protected Refugees
* **Primary Character:** Yveltal
* **Location:** Ruined throne room
* **What Changes:** The god physically enters the arena, bringing catastrophic destruction simply by landing.
* **Scene Action:** Yveltal descends through the shattered ceiling, its massive talons crashing onto the throne room floor. The sheer force of the landing shatters the remaining marble pillars and sends a violent shockwave through the hall. Froslass and the surge-protected refugees are thrown backward by the wind. Yveltal slowly folds its wings, its glowing eyes locking onto Darkedge with cold, calculating observation rather than blind rage.
* **Key Visual:** Yveltal landing heavily in the center of the throne room, kicking up a massive wave of dust and debris that physically pushes the cowering survivors backward.
* **Audience Emotion:** Chilling awe, pure dread.
* **Emotional Transition:** Epic anticipation crystallizing into terrifying reality.
* **Connection from Previous Scene:** Direct continuation of Yveltal's descent in Act 6, Scene 8.
* **Connection to Next Scene:** Prompts Darkedge to immediately attack to draw the god's attention away from the crowd.
* **Connection to Act 8:** Establishes the ruined throne room as an open-air arena.
* **Estimated Duration:** 12 seconds

### Scene 2: The Interception

* **Purpose:** Show Darkedge attacking to protect the survivors, fighting with immense but uncoordinated power, while Yveltal easily evades.
* **Characters:** Darkedge, Yveltal
* **Primary Character:** Darkedge / Yveltal
* **Location:** Ruined throne room
* **What Changes:** The physical clash begins, revealing Darkedge's lack of fusion mastery.
* **Scene Action:** To keep the god away from the refugees, Darkedge launches forward. Its offense is forceful but disjointed—a heavy, flaming blade strike immediately followed by a blast of shadowy energy that disrupts its own momentum. Yveltal does not counterattack; it simply shifts its massive body with minimal, effortless movements, allowing Darkedge's uncoordinated strikes to pass harmlessly by.
* **Key Visual:** Darkedge unleashing a chaotic flurry of violet fire and black shadow, while Yveltal effortlessly tilts and weaves its massive body to evade every strike.
* **Audience Emotion:** Anxiety, realization of the skill gap.
* **Emotional Transition:** Heroic momentum stalling against effortless superiority.
* **Connection from Previous Scene:** Direct response to Yveltal's landing and observation.
* **Connection to Next Scene:** Leads to Darkedge finally landing a hit that Yveltal deliberately allows.
* **Connection to Act 8:** Sets the standard that Darkedge must find a smarter way to fight.
* **Estimated Duration:** 10 seconds

### Scene 3: The God's Indifference

* **Purpose:** Demonstrate the enormous power gap between Darkedge and Yveltal, showing that the fusion's current offensive power cannot meaningfully hurt the god, without establishing absolute invulnerability.
* **Characters:** Yveltal, Darkedge
* **Primary Character:** Yveltal
* **Location:** Ruined throne room
* **What Changes:** Darkedge realizes the terrifying durability of its opponent.
* **Scene Action:** Pressing the assault, Darkedge finally drives a burning violet blade directly against Yveltal's chest. Yveltal stops moving and deliberately allows the strike to connect. The violet fire washes across Yveltal’s feathers without causing any meaningful damage. Realizing the attack accomplished nothing, Darkedge freezes. With a casual, dismissive sweep of its wing, Yveltal bats the fusion violently across the room.
* **Key Visual:** Darkedge's burning violet blade pressed directly against Yveltal’s chest, the god remains complety unmoved allowing the fire to wash over its feathers without any visible damage before casually swatting the darkedge away.
* **Audience Emotion:** Shock, hopelessness.
* **Emotional Transition:** Fleeting hope instantly shut down by an insurmountable power gap.
* **Connection from Previous Scene:** The immediate result of Darkedge's continued assault.
* **Connection to Next Scene:** Darkedge's crash causes catastrophic collateral damage.
* **Connection to Act 8:** Proves Darkedge cannot win a battle of raw attrition.
* **Estimated Duration:** 10 seconds

### Scene 4: The Cost of the Exchange

* **Purpose:** Ground the battle in severe consequences. The destruction threatens the refugees, and the crystal is seamlessly introduced into the rubble without drawing undue attention.
* **Characters:** The Surge-Protected Refugees, Froslass, Darkedge, Yveltal
* **Primary Character:** The Surge-Protected Refugees / Froslass
* **Location:** The central dais (near Solgaleo's statue)
* **What Changes:** Collateral damage causes casualties; the crystal is dislodged and quietly pocketed by a fleeing survivor.
* **Scene Action:** Darkedge crashes into the stonework, sending deadly debris raining over the survivors. Several refugees are struck and killed. The shockwave hurls a wounded survivor directly against Solgaleo's already-fractured petrified statue. The impact cracks the white stone further, dislodging a small, clear, ordinary-looking crystal that falls into the rubble. As Froslass desperately drags another wounded Pokémon to safety, a different fleeing survivor spots the clear crystal and quietly scoops it up while running for cover.
* **Key Visual:** Amid the dust and fleeing refugees, a small clear crystal lies among the rubble at the base of Solgaleo's cracked statue. In the background, a different survivor briefly picks it up while escaping, almost lost among the surrounding chaos.
* **Audience Emotion:** Desperation, grief, claustrophobia.
* **Emotional Transition:** Awe of the gods shifting to the horrifying reality of collateral damage.
* **Connection from Previous Scene:** The direct fallout from Yveltal swatting Darkedge.
* **Connection to Next Scene:** Darkedge recovers, seeing the cost of its failure.
* **Connection to Act 8:** Plants the crystal for its eventual use without explaining its purpose.
* **Estimated Duration:** 11 seconds

### Scene 5: The Unfinished Answer

* **Purpose:** Show Darkedge beginning to adapt and sensing an unstable rhythm, only for Yveltal to immediately interrupt the progress before it can be unleashed.
* **Characters:** Darkedge, Yveltal
* **Primary Character:** Darkedge / Yveltal
* **Location:** Ruined throne room
* **What Changes:** Yveltal actively shuts down Darkedge's attempt to evolve its combat approach.
* **Scene Action:** Seeing the survivors dying, Darkedge recovers and takes a new stance. For a brief second, the violet fire and black shadow along its arms stop clashing and briefly fall into the same rhythm. The resulting energy pattern remains unstable and beyond Darkedge's full control, but the fusion recognizes the momentary synchronization and attempts to direct the unstable energy forward. Before Darkedge can release any technique, Yveltal observes the attempt and immediately changes its behavior. Moving with terrifying, sudden speed, Yveltal lunges and strikes Darkedge heavily, interrupting the progress.
* **Key Visual:** Darkedge’s aura briefly falling into an unstable rhythmic pattern of fire and shadow, only to be violently shattered by Yveltal slamming into it.
* **Audience Emotion:** Frustration, intense thrill.
* **Emotional Transition:** A spark of genuine potential instantly crushed by calculated dominance.
* **Connection from Previous Scene:** Darkedge's desperate attempt to improve after seeing the casualties.
* **Connection to Next Scene:** Yveltal escalates the threat to end the encounter on its terms.
* **Connection to Act 8:** Hints that Darkedge possesses the potential to synchronize, if it can survive long enough to learn.
* **Estimated Duration:** 10 seconds

### Scene 6: The Crimson Sky

* **Purpose:** End the act on a massive, high-stakes cliffhanger. Darkedge is forced to make a desperate choice to shield the survivors from a devastating attack.
* **Characters:** Yveltal, Darkedge, Froslass, The Surge-Protected Refugees
* **Primary Character:** Yveltal / Darkedge
* **Location:** The dark sky / Ruined throne room
* **What Changes:** Yveltal prepares a massive sphere of energy; Darkedge chooses to shield the crowd rather than escape.
* **Scene Action:** Having shut down the fusion's progress, Yveltal ascends high above the ruined throne room. The dark storm clouds swirl as Yveltal gathers a massive sphere of black and crimson energy at its center. The impending attack threatens to engulf the ruined throne room and everyone still inside. Down below, Darkedge is battered and visibly strained. Realizing it cannot evacuate the survivors in time, the fusion positions itself squarely between Yveltal's incoming attack and Froslass's remaining group, crossing its blades in a desperate choice to shield them rather than leave them exposed. The scene cuts to black precisely before the attack makes impact.
* **Key Visual:** Looking up from Darkedge's perspective as it stands protectively before the refugees, while Yveltal charges a colossal black-and-crimson sphere in the sky above.
* **Audience Emotion:** Extreme suspense, breathless dread, protective desperation.
* **Emotional Transition:** Tactical struggle halted by an overwhelming, apocalyptic threat.
* **Connection from Previous Scene:** Yveltal's escalation after interrupting Darkedge's unstable synchronization.
* **Connection to Next Scene:** Bridges directly into Act 8, where the massive attack makes impact.
* **Connection to Act 8:** Continues the confrontation in the partially exposed, ruined throne room, where Yveltal's attack is abount to make impact
* **Estimated Duration:** 10 seconds
