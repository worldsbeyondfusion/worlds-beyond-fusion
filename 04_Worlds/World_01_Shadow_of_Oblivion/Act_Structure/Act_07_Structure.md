# Act 7: The God's Descent

**Status:** PROPOSED DRAFT — NOT CANON

**Target Runtime:** 65–80 seconds

**Planned Scene Action:** 63 seconds (leaves \~2s for visual holds and transitions)

**Scene Count:** 6 scenes

## Act Metadata

* **Act Purpose:** Establish Yveltal’s physical presence and overwhelming power tier. Demonstrate that while Darkedge is vastly superior to the Vanguard, fighting a god is an entirely different scale of combat. Set the stage for a protracted, multi-act boss battle without concluding the fight.

* **Audience Knowledge:** Yveltal has officially entered the battlefield; its mere presence causes the environment to rapidly decay; Darkedge takes the offensive to protect the dais; the fight is evenly matched in speed but Yveltal possesses overwhelming raw power; the battle will require everything Darkedge has just to survive.

* **Primary Character:** Darkedge / Yveltal

* **Secondary Character:** Froslass

* **Threat Presence:** Yveltal (The God of Destruction)

## Scene Breakdown

### Scene 1: The Touchdown

* **Purpose:** Showcase the physical and environmental impact of a god touching down on the battlefield.

* **Characters:** Yveltal, Darkedge

* **Primary Character:** Yveltal

* **Location:** Ruined throne room

* **What Changes:** The battlefield is irrevocably altered by Yveltal's physical landing.

* **Scene Action:** Yveltal descends completely through the shattered ceiling, its massive talons crashing onto the throne room floor. The sheer impact shatters the remaining marble pillars, sending shockwaves that obliterate the lingering dark mist. The crushing atmospheric pressure intensifies, turning the air itself thick and suffocating.

* **Key Visual:** Yveltal landing heavily, its wings fully extended, kicking up a massive cloud of dust and debris as the ground craters beneath it.

* **Audience Emotion:** Chilling awe, pure dread.

* **Emotional Transition:** Epic anticipation crystallizing into terrifying reality.

* **Connection from Previous Scene:** Direct continuation of Yveltal's descent in Act 6, Scene 6.

* **Connection to Next Scene:** Prompts Darkedge to immediately draw aggro away from the dais.

* **Connection to Act 8:** Establishes the ruined throne room as an open-air arena.

* **Estimated Duration:** 12 seconds

### Scene 2: The Interception

* **Purpose:** Show Darkedge taking the initiative to keep the god away from Froslass and Solgaleo's statue.

* **Characters:** Darkedge, Yveltal

* **Primary Character:** Darkedge

* **Location:** Ruined throne room (Airborne)

* **What Changes:** The physical clash between mortal fusion and god begins.

* **Scene Action:** Before Yveltal can turn its gaze toward the center of the dais, Darkedge vanishes in a blur of violet smoke. It reappears instantly in the air directly in front of Yveltal's face, both arm-blades burning with intense ethereal fire. Darkedge swings a massive, cross-shaped strike directly at the god's chest to force it backward.

* **Key Visual:** Darkedge suspended mid-air in front of the colossal Yveltal, unleashing a blazing violet cross-strike.

* **Audience Emotion:** Adrenaline, heroic hype.

* **Emotional Transition:** Dread momentarily pierced by explosive, defiant action.

* **Connection from Previous Scene:** Direct response to Yveltal's landing.

* **Connection to Next Scene:** Demonstrates Yveltal's terrifying durability.

* **Connection to Act 8:** Cements Darkedge's strategy of aerial, high-speed combat.

* **Estimated Duration:** 10 seconds

### Scene 3: The God's Deflection

* **Purpose:** Establish that Yveltal is on a completely different power tier than the Vanguard; Darkedge's attacks will not easily cleave this enemy.

* **Characters:** Yveltal, Darkedge

* **Primary Character:** Yveltal

* **Location:** Ruined throne room

* **What Changes:** Darkedge realizes the scale of durability it is facing.

* **Scene Action:** Darkedge’s flaming violet strike connects, but it does not cut. Instead, a dense, dark crimson aura flares around Yveltal's body, absorbing the blow with a thunderous crack. Yveltal doesn't even flinch. With a casual, almost dismissive sweep of its massive wing, it bats Darkedge out of the air like an insect.

* **Key Visual:** Yveltal's wing glowing with dark red energy as it violently swats the glowing violet form of Darkedge out of the sky.

* **Audience Emotion:** Shock, renewed fear.

* **Emotional Transition:** Heroic hype instantly shut down by overwhelming power.

* **Connection from Previous Scene:** The immediate result of Darkedge's cross-strike.

* **Connection to Next Scene:** Darkedge recovers, but the environment begins to fail.

* **Connection to Act 8:** Sets the standard that Darkedge must find a smarter way to deal damage.

* **Estimated Duration:** 10 seconds

### Scene 4: The Rotting Domain

* **Purpose:** Introduce Yveltal's passive environmental threat—the aura of death.

* **Characters:** Yveltal, Darkedge

* **Primary Character:** Yveltal

* **Location:** Ruined throne room (Arena)

* **What Changes:** The battlefield itself becomes a lethal hazard as it decays.

* **Scene Action:** Darkedge recovers mid-air, landing gracefully on a stone pillar. However, as Yveltal lets out a piercing, unearthly screech, its dark crimson aura expands outward. Everywhere the red light touches, the ancient stone of the kingdom turns grey and crumbles into ash. The pillar beneath Darkedge begins to rapidly dissolve, forcing the fusion to constantly stay on the move just to survive the environment.

* **Key Visual:** A wave of crimson light sweeping across the floor, turning vibrant stone into dead, crumbling ash as Darkedge leaps desperately between falling debris.

* **Audience Emotion:** Desperation, claustrophobia.

* **Emotional Transition:** Direct combat shifting to a desperate struggle against the environment.

* **Connection from Previous Scene:** Escalation of Yveltal's power after swatting Darkedge.

* **Connection to Next Scene:** Forces Darkedge to counter-attack while dodging decay.

* **Connection to Act 8:** The crumbling arena will severely limit Darkedge's movement in the next act.

* **Estimated Duration:** 11 seconds

### Scene 5: The Ethereal Barrage

* **Purpose:** Show Darkedge adapting and fighting back with ranged precision, refusing to be overwhelmed.

* **Characters:** Darkedge, Yveltal

* **Primary Character:** Darkedge

* **Location:** Ruined throne room (Airborne)

* **What Changes:** Darkedge transitions to high-speed ranged combat to avoid the rotting domain.

* **Scene Action:** Realizing it cannot stay grounded, Darkedge blinks rapidly through the air, utilizing Darkrai's levitation. It weaves through the crumbling debris and unleashes a rapid-fire barrage of explosive, violet-and-black energy spheres at Yveltal’s wings. The explosions force Yveltal to finally raise its wings defensively, halting the environmental decay momentarily.

* **Key Visual:** A high-speed tracking shot of Darkedge teleporting through the air, raining down bright violet explosions against Yveltal’s massive dark wings.

* **Audience Emotion:** Thrill, hope for a fair fight.

* **Emotional Transition:** Environmental desperation countered by high-speed tactical offense.

* **Connection from Previous Scene:** Darkedge's adaptation to the crumbling floor.

* **Connection to Next Scene:** Yveltal drops its defense to prepare a devastating counter.

* **Connection to Act 8:** Proves Darkedge can actually hurt or inconvenience the god.

* **Estimated Duration:** 10 seconds

### Scene 6: The Crimson Sky

* **Purpose:** End the act on a massive, high-stakes cliffhanger as Yveltal prepares a god-tier attack.

* **Characters:** Yveltal, Darkedge

* **Primary Character:** Yveltal

* **Location:** The dark sky above the throne room

* **What Changes:** The skirmish escalates into a potentially fatal, massive strike.

* **Scene Action:** Annoyed by the barrage, Yveltal violently snaps its wings open, blowing away the smoke. It ascends high above the ruined throne room. The dark clouds swirl into a vortex around it as Yveltal begins gathering a terrifying, blinding sphere of black and crimson energy at its center. Down below, Darkedge anchors its feet into the remaining stone, crossing its burning violet blades to brace for a world-ending impact.

* **Key Visual:** Looking up from Darkedge's grounded perspective as Yveltal charges a massive, blinding crimson sun of destruction in the dark sky.

* **Audience Emotion:** Extreme suspense, awe, breathless dread.

* **Emotional Transition:** Tactical offense halted by apocalyptic dread.

* **Connection from Previous Scene:** Yveltal's direct retaliation to Darkedge's barrage.

* **Connection to Next Scene:** Bridges directly into Act 8 where this massive attack must be dealt with.

* **Connection to Act 8:** Sets up the opening clash of the next act.

* **Estimated Duration:** 10 seconds
