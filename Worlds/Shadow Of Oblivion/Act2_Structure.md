# Act Structure

## Act Purpose

What must change by the end of this act?
Transform Ceruledge from a returning knight into the guardian of the survivors, complete Solgaleo's sacrifice, and push Darkrai beyond restraint after witnessing the erasure of Togepi.

## Audience Knowledge

What does the audience understand now that the audience did not understand before?
- Ceruledge is loyal to Solgaleo and the kingdom.
- Solgaleo has nearly exhausted himself sustaining the barrier.
- Solgaleo's final power can preserve some beings, but cannot save everyone.
- Darkrai's rage comes from witnessing the destruction of innocence and balance.

## Primary Character

Whose decisions drive this act?
Perspective character: Ceruledge
Decisive character: Solgaleo
Emotional endpoint: Darkrai

## Primary Emotion

What should the viewer mainly feel?
Loyalty, despair

## Scene Limit

Target: 6
Maximum: 8

---

## Scene 1: The Return

Purpose:
Establish urgency and physically carry Ceruledge into the protected kingdom.

Character:
Ceruledge

Location:
Wasteland, weakening barrier, and kingdom entrance

What Changes:
Ceruledge crosses from the erased outer world into the final surviving sanctuary.

Key Visual:
Ceruledge races toward the golden barrier as the reddish-black mist
closes behind, passing through just before the opening seals.

Estimated Duration:
5 seconds

---

## Scene 2: The Exhausted King

Purpose:
Reveal the personal cost of protecting the kingdom.

Character:
Ceruledge and Solgaleo

Location:
Throne room

What Changes:
Ceruledge realizes Solgaleo has been sacrificing his strength for the kingdom.

Key Visual:
Ceruledge enters expecting the magnificent Sun King, but finds Solgaleo
barely standing beneath unstable golden light.

Estimated Duration:
7 seconds

---

## Scene 3: The Silent Witness

Purpose:
Position Darkrai inside the castle before the tragedy and establish
Froslass and Togepi visually.

Character:
Darkrai, Froslass, and Togepi

Location:
Castle corridor leading into the throne room

What Changes:
Darkrai finds the source of the surviving light and sees exactly
what Solgaleo is trying to protect.

Key Visual:
Darkrai watches from a dark corridor while Froslass shields Togepi
near the failing throne room.

Estimated Duration:
5 seconds

---

## Scene 4: The Last Grace

Purpose:
Complete Solgaleo's sacrifice and transfer responsibility to Ceruledge.

Character:
Solgaleo, Ceruledge, Froslass, Togepi, and Darkrai

Location:
Throne room

What Changes:
Solgaleo releases his remaining power, saves those the light can anchor,
and becomes stone.

Key Visual:
Solgaleo's final roar fills the throne room with golden light,
then the light collapses into silence as the Sun King petrifies.

Estimated Duration:
9 seconds

---

## Scene 5: The Empty Embrace

Purpose:
Show the limit of Solgaleo's sacrifice and create the act's emotional valley.

Character:
Froslass and Togepi

Location:
Throne room

What Changes:
Froslass survives, but Togepi is erased.

Key Visual:
The golden light fades. Froslass remains kneeling with both arms
wrapped around empty space as the final particles of Togepi disappear.

Estimated Duration:
7 seconds

## Scene 6 : The Nightmare's Roar

Purpose:
Push Darkrai from controlled anger into open retaliation
and trigger the misunderstanding that begins Act 3.

Character:
Darkrai and Ceruledge

Location:
Throne room and dark corridor

What Changes:
Darkrai loses restraint, while Ceruledge becomes aware of an unknown
shadow presence inside the ruined sanctuary.

Key Visual:
Darkrai emerges from the corridor and unleashes a violent roar.
Ceruledge rises beside Solgaleo's statue and turns toward the sound,
igniting both blades.

Estimated Duration:
5 seconds
