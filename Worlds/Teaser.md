# Worlds Teaser #


### **1. Dark World - Shadow of Oblivion**

The light has failed, leaving only crimson ashes and fading memories in its wake. To save what remains of their shattered realm, they must willingly embrace the void.

### **2. Light World - Radiant Cradle**

When the All-Creator slumbers, an empty throne demands a new, untested Overseer to keep the peace. Strength must be proven and the proud elements united before the golden age can dawn.

### **3. Water World - Abyssal Trench**

Beneath the crushing waves lies a realm of ancient secrets, dormant leviathans, and unforgiving tides. Only those with an unbreakable spirit can navigate the bioluminescent depths and survive the pressure.

### **4. Earth World - Golden Bastion**

Forged in ancient stone and enduring rock, this fortress stands as the ultimate, unyielding shield against the ravages of time. History is written into the very earth here, demanding absolute resilience from those who walk its rigid paths.

### **5. Fire World - Magma Citadel**

A chaotic realm of roaring flames and molten rivers where power is forged in the heart of the volcano. True strength here is not found in absolute destruction, but in the iron will required to tame the inferno.

### **6. Wind World - Zephyr Peak**

High above the clouds, a treacherous gauntlet of floating canyons and roaring updrafts demands unmatched aerial grace. To rule these endless skies, one must learn to outpace the storm and ride the wildest winds.
