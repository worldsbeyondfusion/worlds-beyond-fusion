# AUDIENCE 2: BLULITHE
**The Gentle Shield**

**Fusion:** Growlithe x Swablu  
**Nature:** Gentle, Brave, Loyal  

**Appearance**  
Blulithe is a striking, beautiful fusion of golden puppy and sky-dwelling bird. It possesses the loyal, canine body of a Growlithe, but its mane and tail are made of incredibly soft, bright white Swablu clouds.
A pair of small, fluffy cloud-wings rest on its back.
While it cannot fly high, it can glide gracefully and leap great distances, leaving a faint scent of fresh spring rain and warm embers wherever it lands.

**Personality and Story**  
Blulithe is the anchor of the trio. It has a naturally calm, soothing presence, possessing the gentle heart of a Swablu and the unwavering courage of a Growlithe.
In the Grand Archive, Blulithe is the most attentive listener.
When the Archiver tells tales of the [Light World] and the [Water World], Blulithe sits perfectly still, its cloud-tail wagging softly, completely absorbed by the honor and heroism in the stories.
It understands the emotional weight of the tales more than the other two, often leaning its warm, fluffy head against the Archiver's hand when a story takes a tragic turn.

**Friendship Dynamic**  
Blulithe acts as the protective older sibling of the group.
It is endlessly patient with Togecik's reckless bouncing, often using its soft cloud-wings to catch the little fire-egg mid-air before it crashes into a wall.
For PomFloon, Blulithe is a literal safety net. When PomFloon gets spooked, they immediately hide their face in Blulithe’s thick, cloudy mane.
If a threat ever approaches the trio, Blulithe's gentle demeanor vanishes; it will stand firmly in front of its friends, barking fiercely with a surprisingly deep, booming voice, refusing to back down no matter how big the opponent is.
