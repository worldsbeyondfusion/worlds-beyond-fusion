#### Survival Rule

A Pokemon survives the creeping mist if Dark or Ghost is one of its types.

This includes:
- Pure Dark types.
- Pure Ghost types.
- Dark types with any secondary type.
- Ghost types with any secondary type.

Pokemon without Dark or Ghost typing are erased by the mist.

Use the canonical term:

Dark-type and Ghost-type survivors

Avoid ambiguous terms such as:
- Mixed-element refugees
- Dual-element refugees
- Mixed-type survivors

unless a specific scene requires further classification.
