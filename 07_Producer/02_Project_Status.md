## 5. Locked Disappearance Rule

The creeping dark-red mist naturally spares only:

- Pure Dark-type Pokémon
- Pure Ghost-type Pokémon

Dual-type Pokémon are not naturally protected merely because one of their types is Dark or Ghost.

### Act 1 demonstration

- Poochyena survives because Poochyena is pure Dark.
- Haunter survives because Haunter is pure Ghost.
- Totodile is erased.
- Mimikyu is erased because Mimikyu is Ghost/Fairy rather than pure Ghost.

The audience observes this rule through the events of Act 1. The full mechanic does not need to be explained through exposition in Act 1.

---

## 6. Locked Solgaleo Exception

During Solgaleo's final stretch in Act 2, Solgaleo creates a survival exception.

Solgaleo's final golden surge permanently anchors nearby dual-type Pokémon whose typing includes Dark or Ghost.

This includes:

- Ceruledge as Ghost/Fire
- Froslass as Ice/Ghost
- Nearby Dark-type dual Pokémon
- Nearby Ghost-type dual Pokémon

The visible golden outline is temporary. The anchoring effect is permanent.

Pokémon without Dark or Ghost as one of their types cannot be anchored through this mechanism.

Togepi is pure Fairy. Solgaleo's surge passes over Togepi but cannot form a protective anchor around Togepi's existence.

Togepi is not deliberately excluded. The loss results from the limitation of Solgaleo's protection.

## 14. Current Risks

### Document alignment

The storyline, roadmap, and emotional journey still reflect older architecture and may contradict the current ten-act saga.

### Duplicate source of truth

Older act versions shared in AI conversations may be mistaken for current versions. Every newly supplied file should explicitly supersede earlier versions in the active conversation.

### Status-label drift

AI-generated status labels may incorrectly mark unapproved acts as approved or storyboard-ready.

### Move Canon drift

Unnamed or visually described attacks may become treated as official moves before research and approval.

### Premature production

Starting storyboards, anchors, or video generation before Acts 5 through 10 stabilize would create avoidable rework.

### Crowd continuity

The refugee population in Acts 2 onward will create anchor, positioning, and image-to-video consistency risks.

### Emotional compression

Solgaleo's petrification, Togepi's disappearance, Froslass's empty embrace, and Darkrai's breaking point must retain sufficient silence and visual holds.

### Tool and account reliability

AI usage limits, broken sessions, unavailable notebooks, missing pinned chats, and inconsistent repository retrieval may interrupt continuity. Git and dated producer session logs must remain the recovery system.
