## 3. Revised Story Architecture

### Acts 1-3

The revised opening now establishes:

- Solgaleo witnessing the catastrophe directly.
- Haunter, Totodile, and Mimikyu's bond before Totodile is erased.
- A large surviving refugee population inside the Golden Kingdom.
- Darkrai arriving in a state of tunnel-vision rage toward the external threat.
- Ceruledge misinterpreting Darkrai's behavior.
- Darkrai proving restraint by protecting the refugees.
- Froslass ending the confrontation through emotional consequence.
- Ceruledge and Darkrai forming an uneasy alliance against Yveltal.

### Acts 4-6

The path to Darkedge is now more earned:

```text
Act 4: Ceruledge and Darkrai fight together.
Act 5: Cooperation is not enough, and both sacrifice themselves to protect the survivors.
Act 6: Their shared sacrifice produces Darkedge.
```

Darkedge may dominate the Corrupted Vanguard, but Yveltal must remain a substantially greater threat.

### Acts 7-9

The final escalation is structurally viable:

```text
Act 7: Yveltal demonstrates overwhelming superiority.
Act 8: Darkedge successfully wounds Yveltal.
Act 9: Darkedge realizes Yveltal must be contained rather than defeated conventionally.
```

Acts 7 and 8 are acceptable with modification. Act 9 requires major continuity correction before approval.

## 4. Survival Rule to Standardize

The intended rule is:

> A Pokémon survives the mist if Dark or Ghost is one of its types.

This includes:

- Pure Dark
- Pure Ghost
- Dark/Fire
- Dark/Water
- Dark with any secondary type
- Ghost/Fire
- Ghost/Water
- Ghost with any secondary type

Pokémon without Dark or Ghost typing are erased.

Use the term:

```text
Dark-type and Ghost-type survivors
```

Avoid inconsistent terms such as:

- Mixed-element refugees
- Dual-element refugees
- Mixed-type survivors

The survival rule should be documented once in the relevant world canon or storyline and applied consistently across all nine acts.

## 5. Required Story Corrections

### Refugee Continuity

Act 9 must not call Froslass the sole survivor unless the disappearance of the refugee population is explicitly shown and approved.

Recommended direction:

- The Dark-type and Ghost-type refugees remain alive.
- Froslass becomes the primary keeper of the seal and witness to Darkedge's sacrifice.
- The rescued population inherits the damaged world.

This preserves the payoff of Acts 2-5, where protecting the survivors is the heroes' central motivation.

### Darkedge's Final Fate

The current Act 9 draft contains conflicting outcomes:

- Ceruledge and Darkrai become statues.
- Their souls are consumed.
- Their forms shatter into light.
- A single obsidian seal remains.

One outcome must be selected.

The current scenes most strongly support:

> Ceruledge and Darkrai consume their life forces through Darkedge to create a shared obsidian seal that contains Yveltal.

Update the Act 9 metadata and ending to match that outcome consistently.

### Unapproved Later Acts

Remove references to Acts 10-12 unless Greg explicitly approves another act. A maximum of twelve acts is permission, not a target.

### Core Document Alignment

The storyline, roadmap, and emotional journey still reflect the earlier eight-act version. After the nine-act architecture is stabilized, update those documents to reflect:

- The refugee storyline
- The revised Darkedge formation
- The expanded Yveltal battle
- The nine-act emotional progression
- The containment ending

## 7. Claude Production Workflow

Claude should work in two separate stages.

### Stage 1: Storyboard

Claude converts an approved act structure into shots containing:

- Shot purpose
- Duration
- Subject and composition
- Camera framing
- Character movement
- Emotional intent
- Visual transition
- Anchor-image requirement
- Image-to-video risk

### Stage 2: Prompt Package

Only after the storyboard is reviewed and approved, Claude converts approved shots into:

- Anchor-image prompts
- Image-to-video prompts
- Continuity constraints
- Motion restrictions
- Transition instructions
- Negative prompts where needed

Approved flow:

```text
Approved act structure
→ Claude storyboard
→ Producer feasibility and runtime review
→ Greg approval
→ Claude prompt package
→ Anchor generation
→ Video generation
```

## 8. Transition Ownership

- **Gemini:** Creates narrative and emotional transitions between scenes and acts.
