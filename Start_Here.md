# Worlds Beyond Fusion: Start Here

## Project Purpose

Worlds Beyond Fusion is a cinematic anthology project containing
multiple fusion-based universes and independently themed worlds.

The first active universe is Aetheria.

---

## Fixed Production Format

- 1 world = 1 complete story
- 1 world = exactly 8 acts
- 1 act = 1 published video
- 1 act = 45-60 seconds
- 1 act = 5-8 scenes
- 2 acts published per week
- 1 world published over approximately 4 weeks

These rules are locked unless formally changed through a decision record.
